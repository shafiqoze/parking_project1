Security Awareness Topics

Phishing Awareness

Learn how to identify and protect against email-based attacks.

Warning Signs:

  • Urgent or threatening language
  • Spelling and grammar errors
  • Suspicious sender addresses
  • Requests for sensitive information
  • Unexpected attachments

Password Security

Create and manage strong passwords to protect your accounts.

Best Practices:

  • Use at least 12 characters
  • Mix uppercase and lowercase letters
  • Include numbers and special characters
  • Avoid personal information
  • Use unique passwords for each account

Social Engineering

Protect yourself against manipulation tactics.

Protection Tips:

  • Verify unexpected requests
  • Don't share sensitive information
  • Be wary of impersonators
  • Report suspicious activities
  • Trust your instincts

Safe Browsing

Stay safe while browsing the internet.

Best Practices:

  • Use HTTPS websites
  • Keep software updated
  • Use secure networks
  • Enable firewall protection
  • Avoid suspicious downloads
